# Done When

Repo: https://github.com/fespinoza/DoneWhen

A simple SwiftData + CloudKit app that is like analytics for your personal life.
Where you track `Event` types and their `Occurrences`.

The idea is to be able to answer:

- _How often do I do X?_
- _When was the last time I did X?_

This information is somewhat present in your taks manager, but the focus of
"completed tasks" and "when" that happen, also the tasks may not have the exact
same name. DoneWhen tackles those issues.
